REIT SIM

You are the CEO of a publicly traded Real Estate Investment Trust. Manage your portfolio, survive the board, and grow shareholder value.

How to Play

  • Press Advance Quarter to run each quarter
  • Buy and sell properties to build your portfolio
  • Issue and retire debt — watch your credit rating
  • Set dividends carefully — cutting them angers the board
  • Keep occupancy up, leverage down, FFO growing
  • Board pressure reaches 0/8 — you're fired
  • The world gets harder every year. Survive as long as you can.
