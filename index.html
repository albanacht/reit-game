How a Month Works

  1. Vehicle auctions refresh with new inventory
  2. Private sellers list vehicles
  3. Your staff works (mechanics repair, salesman sells)
  4. Customers arrive based on inventory quality & prices
  5. Sales are calculated with gross margin
  6. Expenses are deducted (payroll, lot fees, utilities)
  7. Debt payments are made
  8. Monthly financial report generated

Employee Performance

Each hire has a skill level (1-10) that improves over time.

Gross Margin %
Percentage profit on each sale. Typical range: 10-25%.
Debt-to-Assets
Leverage ratio. Keep below 70% to stay solvent.
Operating Margin
Net profit as % of revenue. Healthy range: 5-15%.
Gross MarginProfit on each vehicle sale before operating expenses.
Floor PlanDebt used to finance inventory.
AuctionWholesale vehicles. Lower prices, as-is condition.

Budget Strategy

Start by buying cheap used cars at auctions, repair them well, sell at 15-20% margin.
